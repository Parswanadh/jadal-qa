Read this first: the shipped cascade fails out of domain

On 60 real Egocentric-10K windows with human labels (51 good / 9 bad), the shipped IndEgo-calibrated cascade is at chance:

  • score AUROC 0.529 (95% CI 0.32–0.74, window bootstrap, optimistic);
  • it rejected 21 of 51 good windows (41%) and 5 of 9 bad windows;
  • balanced accuracy 0.51.

So the dashboard’s ~40% reject share on Egocentric-10K is mostly false rejects, caused by domain shift (480p/10 fps IndEgo → 1080p/30 fps HEVC factory footage). Verdict shares on that footage are descriptive, not a quality measure.

Source: docs/FACTS.md, “UPDATE 2026-10-10 18:40 IST — corrections”, bullet “OUT-OF-DOMAIN RESULT”. Corroborating registry entry: E6.03 (motion cue frozen out of domain, AUROC 0.281 [0.145, 0.434] — worse than chance, exploratory, n=60/n_bad=9).

Verification note (LEAD B, claim LB1, b/verify, + independent verifier PR #100): these are two different scorers and both numbers are correct. 0.529 is the PoC cascade as shipped (eval/results/poc/ood_check_60.json): AUROC 0.529 [0.316, 0.728], 21 of 51 good windows falsely rejected, 5 of 9 bad, balanced accuracy 0.513 — it reproduces exactly. 0.415 [0.207, 0.630] is the frozen two-view ensemble (caches/ensemble/ood_scores.json, column usable_prob): 26 of 51 good windows rejected, 3 of 9 bad. Both are at chance with heavy false rejects, so the out-of-domain failure is confirmed for both. Do not merge the two figures into one.

1 · What it is, and what decides

Deterministic decision path

  • Measured signals (noise level, sharpness, exposure, frame difference vs the noise floor, residual motion after camera-motion removal, rhythm tests) plus a transparent rule list with exact thresholds.
  • Logistic regression / boosted trees are comparison baselines, not the decision rule.
  • Every reject carries evidence: value, comparison, threshold.
  • Receipts are content-hashed (SHA-256 of canonical JSON), not signed.

Whole video, every frame

  • Per-pixel analysis of every frame, not 4–8 sampled frames.
  • Streaming: 2-second chunks while the worker records; early reject (dark / blocked / frozen) in the first 1–2 s.
  • Humans only audit samples; accept/reject can be fully automated.

Source: docs/FACTS.md, “Direction (decided by the builder today)” and “Pipeline as designed now”.

Pipeline stages

StageScopeWhat it does
0first 1–2 s, tiny resolutionexact rules; reject dark / blocked / over-exposed / frozen immediately, with evidence
1whole video, every frame at reduced resolutionstreaming accumulators: per-pixel spatial spectrum, temporal spectrum, noise model, motion after camera-motion removal → rule list + score

5 · Evidence, cost and hardware (measured)

  • Receipts: SHA-256 of canonical JSON; re-verify = re-run the pinned pipeline on the original clip and compare; a tampered receipt fails. Not cryptographically signed (optional HMAC sidecar).
  • Determinism: CPU feature path gives an identical quantised digest over 10 repeated runs; GPU numerics are not claimed identical across devices; cross-architecture determinism is not claimed.
  • Decode (single core, laptop — not a Pi): full H.264 480p ≈ 90–100× real time; keyframe-only ≈ 200×. On real 1080p/30 fps HEVC: one CPU core decodes 43–55 fps (RTF 1.4–1.8); NVDEC 620–730 fps (RTF 21–24) and saturates at one engine. NVDEC is not faster than CPU decode for a single 480p stream (2,348 vs 2,820 fps).
  • GPU kernels alone: ~700 video-hours per hour at 4.6 GiB (480p ladder; started from 22 h/h on one CPU core). An earlier 1,800–2,000 h/h figure was superseded by this update. End-to-end real throughput is lower (decode-bound) and the end-to-end GPU number is in progress.
  • PoC run on real Egocentric-10K: all 1,546 windows across 58 clips / 4.404 h / 47 factories processed with descriptive verdicts; throughput so far is roughly 7× real time (estimated from windows per elapsed time); the bottleneck is being found.
  • Sampling policy adopted for IndEgo: every 2nd window + 4 s early exit ≈ 0.20 of footage (≈ ×5). Compute accounting: every-2nd → ×1.9; every-2nd + 4 s exit → ≈ ×4.9; every-3rd + 4 s → ×7.2 (decode-bound upper bound). Early exit saturates at 4–6 s (2 s 0.804, 4 s 0.860, 6 s 0.869, 8 s 0.857, full 10.5 s 0.848).
  • Target: 10,000 h in 1–2 h = 5,000× aggregate real time (3,333× at 3 h). With arm B on CPU this needs about 209 (T0) / 263 (T1) cores; moving 4.5–9 TB is likely the bottleneck. Live streaming at the device needs only 1× real time. The builder’s goal is to find the limits of one GPU and show the optimisation ladder — in progress.
  • Hardware: RTX 4070 LAPTOP, 36 SMs / 4,608 CUDA cores, 8 GiB VRAM, ~256 GB/s theoretical bandwidth, PCIe 4.0 x8 (measured host→GPU 5.7–7.6 GB/s). Shared across ~12 jobs through a 3-slot lock; power cap reads 124 W; thermally limited (CPU ~97 °C, GPU 85–90 °C under load).

Sources: docs/FACTS.md, “Measured results so far”, “UPDATE 2026-10-10 17:40 IST”, “UPDATE 2026-10-10 17:50 IST”; registry E3.01–E3.05. The full experiment log with per-id summaries is docs/EXPERIMENTS.md.

6 · What the dashboard is allowed to say

The PoC dashboard reads the real run (1,546 Egocentric-10K windows) and shows verdicts, evidence and receipts. Because the shipped scorer is out-of-domain on that footage, the dashboard shows the “at chance / mostly false rejects” finding on every screen. Verdict rates there are observational counts, not accuracy. Accuracy is reported only where human labels exist (the 150 IndEgo windows) or where a labelled OOD set exists (the 60-window set above).

Sources: docs/FACTS.md, “UPDATE 2026-10-10 18:25 IST” (dashboard on :8840, labelling tool on :8814) and “UPDATE 2026-10-10 18:40 IST”.
