Authoritative list of every experiment in the jadal-qa pod: **42 entries** covering
`E0.01`–`E9.01`, the `LB1`–`LB5` verification/lead-B track, and the `RES` / `SKIP` / `MR`
follow-ups (agents X1–X3). Machine-readable copy: [`docs/experiments/index.csv`](experiments/index.csv).
Term definitions: [`docs/GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md).

Sources (all read-only): the union registry
`/workspace/wt/b-submission-docs/experiments/registry.jsonl`; per-branch registries and
reports under `origin/<branch>`; `docs/reports/*.md`; `/workspace/jadal_pod_bundle/docs/FACTS.md`
and `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`; `docs/reports/branch_triage.md`; open PR titles from `gh pr list`.
Every row cites a report and/or a result JSON. Anything not yet delivered is marked
**PENDING** — never guessed.

Verdict legend: **adopt** (ship it) · **maybe** (kept as prior/lead, not shipped as-is) ·
**drop** (measured negative, do not ship) · **inconclusive** (blocked on missing data) ·
**PENDING** (work not delivered at this writing).

## Headline adopted stack (IndEgo)

- Sampling: **every-2nd window** + **4 s early exit** (E3.01, E3.03, E3.05; ~x4.9 cost cut).
- Views: **EG2 window embedding** (~0.85) and **26 per-pixel features** (~0.81), equal-weight
  fused (~0.87 at the 4 s prefix, LB2); verified independently at 20/20 exact (LB1, E9.01).
- Regimes: **deterministic descriptor router + 5 profiles** (E4.04); **no per-regime model**
  (E4.02 drop).
- Dropped: mosaic-as-model-input (E2), frame selection (E1.04), CUSUM sampler / skip-first-M
  (E3), per-regime models (E4.02), re-encode-as-transfer-fix (E4.03), motion-cue as an
  independent signal (E6, all four).
- Open/PENDING: OOD transfer (IndEgo-trained models fail, 0.4-0.53; E5), 160-window
  confirmatory OOD (`labels2` missing; E5.04 / LB4), and RES / SKIP / MR (X1-X3 not
  delivered). The scale re-score (E8.01, PR #113) finished as a descriptive contract only.

## Notes on sources and honesty

- **Union registry first.** Verdicts and headlines are taken from the union registry
  (`/workspace/wt/b-submission-docs/experiments/registry.jsonl`), which consolidates the
  per-branch registries. Where a branch registry is newer (E2 finished verdicts on
  `feat/exp-mosaic`; E1.05 on `feat/exp-fps-pod`; E5.00 on `feat/exp-domain-adapt`), the
  branch value is used and the report/PR cell names that branch.
- **E2** is registered as `running` in the union registry but is **finished and pushed** on
  `feat/exp-mosaic` (PR #95); the finished per-id verdicts are used here.
- **PENDING X1-X3.** `RES` (X1) had no delivered report/result at this writing (sweep
  running). `SKIP` (X2) and `MR` (X3) have **uncommitted** result JSONs in their worktrees
  but no report, branch commit or PR; their observed values are shown flagged as
  uncommitted and must not be cited as final.
- **OOD caveat, every time.** Any Egocentric-10K number has `n_bad=9` (exploratory) and an
  optimistic window-bootstrap CI; IndEgo-trained models did not transfer (0.4-0.53 AUROC),
  so PoC/E8 verdict shares are **descriptive, not a quality measure**.
- **Two OOD numbers, two scorers.** 0.529 = PoC cascade; 0.415 = frozen ensemble
  `ensemble_v1` (LB1 finding, `docs/reports/pod_verification.md`). Do not merge them.
- **One human labeller, 7 clips, ~3 wearers** on IndEgo (effective n ≈ 11-43); no certified
  error bounds at n=150. Treat all CIs as wide.
