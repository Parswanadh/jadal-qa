## Headline adopted stack (IndEgo)

- Sampling: **every-2nd window** + **4 s early exit** (E3.01, E3.03, E3.05; ~x4.9 cost cut);
  **no section-skip policy** beats it (SKIP: uniform-every-2 stays best at 51.5% cost /
  0.049 error, 7/7 verdicts).
- Views: **EG2 window embedding** (~0.85) and **26 per-pixel features** (~0.81), equal-weight
  fused (~0.87 at the 4 s prefix, LB2); **rank-average** of the same two views is a free
  upgrade (Y3.02: 0.834 -> 0.844, profile-band mass 0.653 -> 0.607). Verified independently
  at 20/20 exact (LB1, E9.01).
- Regimes: **deterministic descriptor router + 5 profiles** (E4.04); **no per-regime model**
  (E4.02 drop).
- Transport: **360p@CRF28** shipping point (RES; 0.8036 at 257 KiB, ~40% of the 480p@CRF23
  bytes); resolution is a bytes lever, and 240p/180p/120p all hold within 0.02 of the 480p
  native baseline (0.810).
- Review reduction (workload, not accuracy): raw abstain band **w=0.16 -> 53.4%** review
  (Y1.01); cost-margin **gating coverage 0.45 + stage-0 -> 54.0%** review at gold BA 0.837
  (Y2.01/Y2.02), down from the frozen E8.01 **75.5%**.
- Dropped: mosaic-as-model-input (E2; X3/MR keeps the E2 verdict), frame selection (E1.04),
  CUSUM sampler / skip-first-M (E3), per-regime models (E4.02), re-encode-as-transfer-fix
  (E4.03), motion-cue as an independent signal (E6, all four), section-skip-anywhere (SKIP),
  stacking extra cached views (Y3.02), unsupervised CORAL/whitening/per-clip norm as a
  transfer fix (Z1.02), a 5% 1-delta CRC bound on 7 clips (Z2.03), sequential-SPRT review
  schedules cheaper than every-2nd (Z4.02), and the frozen ensemble as a standalone OOD
  scorer (O1.01: 0.566, CI contains 0.5).
- Review with a stated bound (Z2.01, adopted workload rule): split-conformal at coverage
  0.85 cuts review 75.5% -> **12.0%** with a marginal false-decision bound <= 15% and
  empirical marginal false-accept 4.0% (IndEgo gold LOCO, n=150, human). Per-clip fairness
  needs Mondrian-by-clip (Z2.02, maybe); a 5% CRC bound is infeasible on 7 clips (Z2.03, drop).
- OOD transfer remains exploratory (Z1/Z3, all OOD n_bad=9): label-free best is the
  **sharpness-3 head at 0.706 [0.518,0.867]** (Z1.01, maybe) and the per-domain-z
  transferable-6 at **0.852 [0.732,0.950]** (Z3.03, maybe, transductive); ~12-24 OOD
  labels recover 0.72-0.76 post-hoc (Z1.03, maybe). Nothing here is a transfer rate.
- 160-label OOD confirmation: frozen ensemble **0.566** (grouped CI 0.414-0.697, contains
  0.5; O1.01, drop as a standalone scorer); review can go <= 60% with no refit
  (O3.02 adopted workload rule: rank-avg + band w=0.155 -> **42.8%** review at BA 0.708,
  but the accuracy is not established). Clip-level SPRT does not beat every-2nd (Z4.02,
  drop); the factory-covering next-100 label queue ships (Z4.03, adopt).
- Open/PENDING: **O2** (E5.04-final + few-shot curve on the 160 labels, `m/fewshot160`,
  PR #130, still running) and **O4** (still running, no PR at this writing). The scale
  re-score (E8.01, PR #113) finished as a descriptive contract only
  (accept 19.2% / reject 5.4% / review 75.5%).

## Notes on sources and honesty

- **Union registry first.** Verdicts and headlines are taken from the union registry
  (`/workspace/wt/b-submission-docs/experiments/registry.jsonl`), which consolidates the
  per-branch registries. Where a branch registry is newer (E2 finished verdicts on
  `feat/exp-mosaic`; E1.05 on `feat/exp-fps-pod`; E5.00 on `feat/exp-domain-adapt`), the
  branch value is used and the report/PR cell names that branch.
- **E2** is registered as `running` in the union registry but is **finished and pushed** on
  `feat/exp-mosaic` (PR #95); the finished per-id verdicts are used here.
- **X1–X3 and Y1–Y3 are final.** `RES` (X1, PR #117), `SKIP` (X2, PR #114) and `MR` (X3,
  PR #119) delivered reports, result JSONs and committed images; the Y review-reduction
  follow-ups (`Y1.01` #118, `Y2.01`/`Y2.02` #116, `Y3.01`/`Y3.02` #120) are folded in from
  the X/Y state reports (`/workspace/state/EXP_*_REPORT.md`) and branch result JSONs. Nothing
  in the table is uncommitted worktree noise anymore.
- **Z1–Z4 and O1/O3 are measured but unmerged.** Their result JSONs, reports and figures
  live on their branches (`m/domain-adapt`, `m/conformal`, `m/new-features`,
  `m/clip-sequential`, `m/ood160-eval`, `m/review160`); the report/result/image cells use
  a `branch:path` prefix to say so. Headline numbers are taken from the authoritative
  state summaries (`/workspace/state/EXP_ADAPT_REPORT.md`, `EXP_CONFORMAL_REPORT.md`,
  `EXP_FEATURES_REPORT.md`, `EXP_CLIP_REPORT.md`, `EXP_OOD160_REPORT.md`,
  `EXP_REVIEW160_REPORT.md`). **O2** (`m/fewshot160`, PR #130) and **O4** are still
  running: their rows are PENDING and cite no numbers.
- **Review-share numbers are workload.** The 75.5% E8.01 review share (and the Y1/Y2/Y3
  reductions) are computed on 1,546 **unlabelled** windows (`label_source: none`): they are
  descriptive, not accuracy. The `[0.25,0.75]` profile band — not `[0.4,0.6]` — is what
  produces the 75.5% (Y3 honesty note).
- **OOD caveat, every time.** Any Egocentric-10K number on the 60-window set has `n_bad=9`
  (exploratory) and an optimistic window-bootstrap CI; on the 160-window set `n_bad=22`
  with the honest factory-grouped bootstrap (still wide; O1.01 grouped CI 0.414-0.697).
  IndEgo-trained models did not transfer (0.4-0.53 AUROC at n=60; frozen 0.566 at n=160,
  CI contains 0.5), so PoC/E8 verdict shares are **descriptive, not a quality measure**.
- **Two OOD numbers, two scorers.** 0.529 = PoC cascade; 0.415 = frozen ensemble
  `ensemble_v1` (LB1 finding, `docs/reports/pod_verification.md`). Do not merge them.
- **One human labeller, 7 clips, ~3 wearers** on IndEgo (effective n ≈ 11-43); no certified
  error bounds at n=150. Treat all CIs as wide.
