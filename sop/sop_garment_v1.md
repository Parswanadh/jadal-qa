# Garment QA SOP — T-shirt collar attach

## Step 1 — Setup and safety

Clear the bench before you start. Check that the machine is threaded, the
needle is straight and the bobbin has enough thread. Wear your ID card and
keep the cloth stack on your left. Report a broken needle or a slipping belt
to the supervisor immediately.

## Step 2 — Mark and cut the collar pieces

Lay the collar pattern on the folded fabric with the grain running the short
way. Cut two collar pieces per garment. Check every piece for frayed edges
and throw damaged pieces in the reject bin, not the good stack.

## Step 3 — Attach the collar to the neck

Fold the seam allowance once and press it. Match the centre mark of the
collar with the back neck centre mark, then pin both collar points to the
shoulder seams. Sew the collar in one pass at 12 to 14 stitches per inch,
stopping 2 cm before the point to lift the presser foot and turn.

## Step 4 — Trim, turn and press

Trim the collar seam allowance down to 3 mm, taking care not to cut the
stitching. Turn the collar to the right side through the gap left at the back
neck. Push every corner out fully and press the collar flat with the iron set
to cotton.

## Step 5 — Final check and pack

Hold the garment by both collar points and check they are the same length.
Look for skipped stitches, loose threads and shading between the two collar
pieces. Anything you would not buy yourself goes to the rework basket with a
reason tag. Fold the finished garment, attach the tag and place it in the pack
bin.
