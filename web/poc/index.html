Finding (human-labelled check, 2026-10-10): on 60 random real Egocentric-10K windows labelled by a human (51 good, 9 bad; exploratory, only 9 bad), the PoC cascade as shipped is at chance: score AUROC 0.529 (0.32–0.74 by window, optimistic), balanced accuracy 0.51. It rejected 21 of 51 good windows (41%), so the ~40% reject share on this footage is mostly false rejects from the 480p/10 fps → 1080p/30 fps domain shift.

Verdict rates on Egocentric-10K are descriptive, not quality. Accuracy on IndEgo (150 windows, AUROC ~0.7–0.8, not yet high) is a different data set. Thresholds are not certified; not measured on a Raspberry Pi; receipts are content-hashed, not signed. Full numbers: claims table.
